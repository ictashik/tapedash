Manual: in neutral, hold throttle to rev the engine; shift to 1 and ease on the throttle to pull away. Shifts take a quarter of a second with the clutch out, so the revs drop and catch as in a car. Lift off in gear to hear the overrun; hit the limiter at the top.
This is the app's own synth (lib/sound/engine_synth.dart), rendered by tool/sound_lab.dart and streamed here. A short, constant lag (about a tenth of a second) between a key and the sound is the stream, not the engine.
