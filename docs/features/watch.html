Features / Other screens and your data

The watch

TapeDash also runs on an Android watch, on its own screens, and talks to the adapter itself. A round Drive face, Money, Where it went and a menu wheel, turned by the bezel. What does not fit a wrist stays on the phone.

  • Simulator
  • Wear OS 3 emulator
  • On a real watch: not yet

A second screen, on your wrist

The phone's screens, made small, do not fit a watch

The same app ran on a Galaxy Watch4 Classic and connected and drove, but the screens were a phone's and did not fit a circle. So the watch has its own surface, chosen when the system says the device is a watch or its shorter side is under 300 lp. It talks to the adapter itself, and everything that does not fit a wrist stays the phone's.

The reel

Four faces, turned by the bezel

A reel of faces turned by the rotating bezel, a vertical swipe or a tap. While the car moves it reaches two faces, and while parked four. Back does nothing while moving.

The wheel

Settings as a wheel, with the bezel

The menu is a wheel: connect, speed from, the price as a dial, look, data, end trip and about. Turn the bezel to move through it.

First run

Set up on the watch, or from your laptop

Typing a setup on a watch is awful, so a setup can instead be sent from your laptop as a file: the adapter, the source and the fuel price arrive in one line of an exchange file.

What stays on the phone

Left out on purpose

No HUD, engine sound, Review, Coach, History, Insights, Diagnostics or Pit Stop on the watch. They do not fit a wrist, and they belong to the phone. The watch starts at the lowest fidelity tier and is held to Balanced, and its heat warnings come earlier, at 41 and 45 degrees.

Said plainly

Only an emulator has seen it

Seen

  • The faces rendered at 192, 198, 225, 240 and 280 lp in circular-masked sheets, with a test that fails on any lit pixel outside the circle or a line of text over the rim.
  • The whole app on a simulated car at those sizes with the bezel, the wheel, the menu, the price dial and an export.
  • An APK built, installed and driven on a Wear OS 3 emulator with a 454 px round panel: the first run, a setup pushed over adb, the drive over WiFi to the simulator, and an export read back on the laptop.

Not yet

  • A real watch: the rotating bezel, orientation, Back and Home, and battery and heat on a 250 to 400 mAh cell with the screen kept on.
  • The wrist test in sunlight on an AMOLED.
  • How Wear OS treats an app that keeps the screen awake through a drive, in ambient mode and in the background.
  • Not built: the dim after 60 seconds parked, and the car's fuel, tank and price as dials in the connect flow.

TapeDash is a free, open-source, strictly offline Android app. Fuel and money figures are estimates. It is an aid, not a certified instrument: set it up while parked, and you remain responsible for driving safely.
