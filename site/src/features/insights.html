Parked only

Nine things the trips can tell you

Insights opens from the dock when you are parked and closes itself the moment the car moves. Every one is an estimate made from what the adapter already reports and the trips already recorded, and each page says what it is made from. The pictures on this page are drawn by the real app from made-up driving in the test harness, so read the shapes and not the numbers.

Honest about the method

Wheel power is worked out from speed and acceleration, with no allowance for hills, so Power and Powerflow are estimates and say so. The first model read one wrong speed reading (46 then 95 km/h) as 100 kW, so it now takes a median of three readings, fits a slope, drops impossible moments, and reports the edge's peak as the highest seen.

The year

Year on Tape, and a picture to share

Every trip of the year is a bar of one barcode, stacked by where the money went. One Share button makes a 1920 by 1080 picture, drawn by the app in its own fonts, and hands it to Android's share sheet. The app adds no permission for it and uploads nothing. What you pick in the sheet is your choice.

Said plainly

What has been seen

Seen

  • Every analysis in tests, with a made-up commute fixture and a plausibility check against the real recordings.
  • The real recordings of a real car: the gearbox comes out a fan because it is a CVT, and the wheels get 7 to 17% of the fuel's energy.
  • Every tab at 640, 720 and 800 wide, and the page opening and closing in the app shell.

Not yet

  • The Android share sheet. It compiles and the Dart side is tested on a mocked channel, but nobody has watched it open on a phone.
  • The counted tank on a real car. It starts only after a full tank is logged and drifts if trips are missed.
  • Toll Map and Ghost commute on a phone's real GPS scatter. The thresholds (150 m for a place, 180 m for a route) are guesses until a real week is in.
  • A map under the Toll Map, so places are numbered rings and not names.
