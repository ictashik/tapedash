When the engine goes off

A trip becomes a receipt, a route and a replay

Every trip is kept on the phone with a sample each second: speed, revs, throttle, fuel use, GPS position, and where the car reports them, load, coolant and battery voltage. Review is parked only.

The receipt

Rupees, distance, time, economy, rupees a kilometre and idling, with the four buckets of where it went.

A route coloured by what you choose

Speed, revs, throttle, load, coolant, volts or rupees a kilometre. The scrubber beneath follows it and the cursor says its value.

Moments marked on the way

Hard braking and hard acceleration appear on the route and on the scrubber, so you can jump to them.

One tip, at most two

Chosen by rupee impact, and only here, never while driving.

The Debrief

A few seconds that close the trip

When the engine has been off long enough to end the trip, the Debrief plays: the route draws itself and the figures count up to the receipt. A touch skips it, and if the engine comes back it is put away and the trip carries on.

Replay

Drive it again, at 1x, 2x or 3x

A play button, a speed button and a stop button sit beside the scrubber. The route turns into a game map: a chase camera follows an arrow for the car, zooms out as the car speeds up and in as it slows, and the map turns with it, or stays north up. The route behind the car is bright and the route ahead is dim.

  • The car stands still while the car stood, placed by the trip's odometer and never by time, and a stretch with the engine off is crossed in half a second.
  • It ends on the receipt's own totals, so the replay and the receipt never disagree.
  • The optional engine sound plays from the recorded revs, a moment ahead of the picture.
  • Pinch to zoom from 1 to 24 times, drag to pan, double-tap to zoom, and tap the route to put the cursor there.

On the phone, and only there

Trips live in a database on the phone. They go nowhere unless you export them yourself as a file (see Garage, exchange and survey).

No base map, on purpose

There is the route and no map under it. A real map from a file you put on the phone has been assessed and left for later: the usual map library needs an HTTP client, which the offline rules ban, so it would need a vector-tile reader of our own.

Said plainly

What has been seen

Seen

  • Review on a real phone, with a real trip, on the first real drive.
  • All of it laid out at 640, 720 and 800 wide in tests, with the Debrief playing, skipping and being put away, and tips never shown while the vehicle moves.
  • Trip boundaries on a real recording: one ignition-off in a real drive ended the trip and the restart three minutes later resumed the same one, as designed.

Not yet

  • The replay and the map gestures by a finger on a phone. They have been driven by tests and looked at in captures only.
  • A very long trip zoomed in. The route is thinned to about 1,000 points, so a deep zoom shows straight runs and not the road.
