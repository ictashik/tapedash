One choice, kept

You choose the source, and it stays chosen

The first screen asks: CONNECT MY CAR or GPS SPEED ONLY. The answer is kept. An adapter that will not connect stays a drive that is looking for its adapter, and never quietly turns into the GPS. Garage, Speed from, switches between the two. There is no demo mode: the app never shows simulated data as if it were your car.

What it will and will not say

Honest about what a receiver knows

The receiver's own speed

The speed is the Doppler speed the receiver reports. Below about 1.5 km/h it reads zero, so a parked car shows 0 and not a drifting 2.

Distance from speed

The distance is that speed over time and never the sum of position jumps, which would add up GPS noise as if it were road.

Poor fixes are ignored

A fix less accurate than 100 m is not used at all.

Nothing invented

No tach, money, coach, trips or engine sound, because nothing reads them. The HUD works too, with the speed alone.

What has been tested

Seen

  • The rules in tests: a simulated city drive whose distance comes out within 3% of the simulated car's own ground truth.
  • The whole app on a fake receiver at 640, 720 and 800 wide, in widget tests.
  • An Android 16 emulator with the receiver moving at 30 km/h, as the source for the navigation HUD, in a release build.

Not yet

  • GPS speed on a real phone in a real car.
  • The screen at the oldest-phone frame budget, which has not been timed.
  • A page for it in the Design Lab. It is exercised by a capture test only, and has no golden image yet.
